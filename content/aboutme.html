About the Site

This site was created as a place to host and share my photos, and some of my findings with tornados. It is essentially just a glorified blog site. I do plan on adding tools and other helpful things eventually to the site.

Who Am I?

I am a young person, with an (unhealthy) obsession with Meteorology and Photography. I have documented severe weather in my local area since 2022, and you can find me infront of a storm in Southern Ontario.
