What is a Radar?

The concept behind a radar is quite simple. It is very similiar to how bats find their surroundings using echo-location. But in this case, we are just using radio waves instead of sound waves. The radar sends out a radio wave pulse, and then listens to how much of that pulse got reflected back. Now, different materials send back more or less of the wave. Light rain, bugs, and even wood has a low "reflectivity" (the amount of the radio wave that got reflected back, which I will refer to it as), whereas things like metal, buildings, and the ground have a high "reflectivity".

Uses

Radar has many uses, from searching for aircraft, to catching speeding. But the main thing that I am going to focus on in this page is on weather radars.
