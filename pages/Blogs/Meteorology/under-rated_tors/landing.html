About This

This is a page/series of pages talking about different tornadoes that could have, or should have been rated F5 or EF5. I will not be using ONLY recorded Doppler windspeeds as a justification. If other damage, or contextual damage, is present, and has recorded doppler winds recorded, then it will be used. I wont be basing my claims on just that alone.

This will also be updated when needed, and will include tornadoes I see fit, or find interesting enough, or dealt enough damage to be included on here.
