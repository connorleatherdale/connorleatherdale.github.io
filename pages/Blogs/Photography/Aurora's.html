The Aurora's

Just a note: this page will be updated as I catch more aurora's

2024

I managed to snag a few photos of the Aurora's in 2024. I was lucky enough to be able to watch them from my back deck at our house. From what i can remember is that they were just slightly visable with my eyes. I could see just the hint of red in teh sky to my north. There was some light pollution to my east, and from the town behind me, but luckily, my deck faces inbetween those. Even on a clear night, you can see alot of stars and satellites (perks of living in the county). In the second photo, you can actually see a satellite in the sky go across the sky (or a plane, theres several major airports near where I live.).
